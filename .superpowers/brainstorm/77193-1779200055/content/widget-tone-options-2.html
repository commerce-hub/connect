Halo Surface

The most modern while staying practical: more energy than flat enterprise, less risk than full glass. Good if you want the widget to feel “new” out of the box.

Recommendation: Precision Layered if you want the safest base, or Halo Surface if you want the default widget to feel more modern and differentiated.
